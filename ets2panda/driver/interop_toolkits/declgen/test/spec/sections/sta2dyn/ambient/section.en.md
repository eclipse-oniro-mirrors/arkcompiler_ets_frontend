**Introduction**

The output of Declgen from ArkTS-Sta to the ArkTS-Dyn context must conform to the **Ambient Declaration** specification of ArkTS-Dyn.

For details, see [TypeScript: Documentation - Declaration Reference](https://www.typescriptlang.org/docs/handbook/declaration-files/by-example.html).
